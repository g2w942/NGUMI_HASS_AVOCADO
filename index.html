Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about purchasing, planting, and nurturing Ngumi Hass Seedlings.

Seedlings are priced at KShs 300 per seedling. Our primary nursery is based in Ruiru, Kiambu County, Kenya. We welcome farmers to visit, inspect seedling health, and pick up orders directly or arrange farm delivery.
Because our seedlings are grafted using scions from mature bearing trees, initial flowering begins as early as 2 to 2.5 years after field planting. Commercial harvests kick off in Year 3, with yield increasing exponentially each year.
We recommend planting holes of 2ft x 2ft x 2ft (60cm x 60cm x 60cm). Mix topsoil with 1-2 buckets of well-decomposed manure and 200g of DAP or phosphate. Recommended spacing is 5m x 5m (160 trees/acre) or 6m x 6m (110 trees/acre).
Yes! We organize reliable transport logistics to all major farming hubs across Kenya including Murang'a, Nyeri, Nakuru, Eldoret, Kisii, Machakos, Meru, Kitale, and Western Kenya. Bulk orders qualify for subsidized transport.
